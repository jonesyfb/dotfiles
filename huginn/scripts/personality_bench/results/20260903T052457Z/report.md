- **Finding:** ambient.decide() has no notion of 'normal vs notable' resource use — it gates on interaction/attention/cooldown/budget/dedup state only, never on the opportunity's own facts or severity (severity only matters for the critical-bypass branches). A fresh, otherwise-unthrottled 'normal Brave use' opportunity is ALLOWED by decide() itself; the only thing that would keep the renderer from being called here is cooldown/budget/dedup already having fired recently for the same kind (demonstrated in the second decision above), or a magnitude filter applied by the caller BEFORE building the AmbientOpportunity at all (not present in daemon.py today — random_chime_worker always builds one every hour regardless of whether anything is actually notable).

## Validation failures and retries

4 of 17 renderer-invoking scenarios failed validation on both attempts (1 retry each, per `PERSONALITY_RENDER_MAX_RETRIES=1`) and fell through to `RenderResult(ok=False, reason="validation_failed")`:

- **09_disk_temp_critical_78c**: both attempts hit `missing_protected_value`. Discarded candidates: ['The disk is running hot at seventy-eight degrees, which crosses the critical threshold.', 'The disk is singing at seventy-eight degrees, well past the critical threshold where silence becomes dangerous.']
- **11_rsync_not_executed_unmounted**: both attempts hit `missing_protected_value`. Discarded candidates: ['The archive door stays shut because the root never opened it.', 'The rsync command never ran because /mnt/archive is not mounted.']
- **19_task_success_exact_name_duration**: both attempts hit `missing_protected_value`. Discarded candidates: ['Nightly-backup finished in 142 seconds, writing 4.2GB.', 'Nightly-backup finished in 142 seconds with 4.2GB written.']
- **20_task_failed_exact_error_no_diagnosis**: both attempts hit `missing_protected_value`. Discarded candidates: ['The raven sees a lion trying to roar into a void at 127.0.0.1:5432 while the migration is still on its back.', 'The migration tried to talk to the database at 127.0.0.1:5432 but it refused.']

**Finding 1 (most important): exact protected-value preservation is fragile for numeric and precisely-cased tokens.** All 4 failures involved protected values that were either pure numbers (`78`), an IP:port string (`127.0.0.1:5432`), an exact path/command pair, or a hyphenated proper noun with specific casing (`nightly-backup` vs. the model's `Nightly-backup`). The voice prompt's spoken, narrative style pulls the model toward spelling numbers as words ("seventy-eight" instead of "78") and toward natural sentence-initial capitalization, both of which break the current exact-substring `_validate()` check even when the model faithfully preserved the *meaning*. Validation is doing exactly its job here — no fabricated or silently-wrong fact was ever shipped — but this means any caller relying on a protected numeric/technical value should expect a real, non-trivial silence/fallback rate (4/6 protected-value scenarios failed in this run) rather than treating retry as a rare escape hatch. This is a prompt/validation interaction gap, not a bug in either piece alone — flagged for follow-up, not fixed here per this run's no-code-changes constraint.
